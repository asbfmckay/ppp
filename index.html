Guide & settings

Setup & guide

Everything you need to run the plan — and to connect Skylight — is here. Tap a section to open it.

  1. Set up your support crew. A "three-legged stool": your employer (financial wellbeing & development), a mentor or family member (accountability and an outside view), and others as needed. Tell each who else is involved — be transparent.
  2. Five-Point Reset. Honestly assess your current state across the five pillars. Do this first; it sets the direction for everything else.
  3. Goals & habits. Turn your toughest reset areas into up to five SMART goals, then set the daily/weekly habits that close the gap.
  4. Financial tracker. Use a budgeting app or bank feed for day-to-day tracking; use this app's Money snapshot for reviews. Score the PPP checklist out of 44.
  5. First meeting. Share your plan with the crew, check your goals are realistic, and agree the first actions.
  6. Set a cadence. Meet monthly when working through challenges, quarterly when tracking well. Review the plan before each meeting.

Plan — your purpose, values and ultimate goal. The anchor everything serves.

Reset — current state across the five pillars. A living document; revisit monthly or after a setback.

Goals — your future state. Pick a horizon (quarterly/annual/3-year) and add milestones; progress fills in as you tick them. The overview grid shows progress per pillar.

Habits — the chain. Daily, weekly or monthly. Tap to keep it unbroken, link each to a goal, and mirror from Skylight via the routine field.

Money — checklist score, a simple budget (surplus/deficit) and net worth. No forecasting — keep the full PPP Excel tool for retirement and loan modelling.

More — this guide, the Skylight connection, and backup/print/clear.

Each completed day adds a link; consecutive days merge into a solid chain. The whole game is simple: don't break the chain.

  • A miss dents your current chain, but your best chain still stands — so avoid all-or-nothing thinking. Just start a fresh link tomorrow.
  • Start with one or two habits. Add more only once they're sticking.
  • Use habit-stacking — attach a new habit to one you already do (e.g. "after coffee, read 10 minutes").
  • Mirrored (Skylight) habits fill themselves on sync; manual habits you tap here.
Meetings

Keep the cadence (monthly/quarterly), report progress honestly, and accept blunt feedback — accountability is the bridge between intention and action.

Goals

Five or fewer. Make them SMART, begin with the end in mind, and break each into milestones so progress is visible.

Habits

Small, stacked and consistent. The unbroken chain beats occasional intensity. If fitness is already strong, point a habit at a weaker area.

Money

Live within your means, keep an emergency fund, avoid high-interest debt, and consider putting extra off the mortgage when you're in surplus. This app isn't financial advice.

Family & data

Share finances monthly and give every member a savings plan. Each person can keep their own exported file. Export a backup regularly — data lives only in this browser.
